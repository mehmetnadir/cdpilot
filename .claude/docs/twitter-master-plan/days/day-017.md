# Day 017 — T2 Anti-Bot Wars

## Topic
Browser history simulation

## Tweet 1/3 — Hook
Anti-bots check `window.history.length`. Land on a deep product page with a history depth of 1 and no referrer header — you're not a user, you're a script that appeared out of thin air. Is your automation actually building a session history?

## Tweet 2/3
Advanced behavioral scoring looks at navigation patterns. cdpilot can warm up a context: hit the search engine, then the homepage, then category pages, then the target. Adds ~200-500ms but is the difference between passing and getting blocked on high-security sites.

## Tweet 3/3 — Anchor
Also: `Page.navigateToHistoryEntry` lets you simulate the back button. A human who navigates wrong, goes back, and retries — that's a strong signal. A bot that goes A → B → C → done is suspicious. Simulate the wrong turns.
